03

Pause-Aware Language Modeling with Calibrated Multimodal Fusion for Alzheimer's Disease Detection

ATP-CALF-AD: a three-pathway multimodal fusion pipeline for Alzheimer's detection from speech. Fuses acoustic features (Whisper + BiLSTM + Attention), text semantics (ERNIE-Large), and pause-augmented text (ERNIE-Base) with Platt-scaled probability calibration before late fusion. Evaluated on DementiaBank Pitt Corpus against four recent SOTA baselines.
